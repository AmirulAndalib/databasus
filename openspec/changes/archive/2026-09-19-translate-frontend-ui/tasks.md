## 5. Slice migration (English and Russian together)

Ordering is deliberate and not by size alone. `features/settings` comes first to prove the infrastructure on the smallest slice. `features/databases` comes second, out of size order, because it is the largest and most varied slice: if the key namespace design breaks anywhere it breaks there, and finding that out after nine migrated slices would mean reworking all of them. The cost is doing the hardest slice before the pattern is fluent. The rest then run ascending.

A slice is done when:

- its copy is in the dictionaries, following the `frontend/AGENTS.md` convention from 2.3;
- its consumers of the section 4 tables use keys, and any old label function or copy whose last consumer it held is deleted;
- its `fromNow()` calls go through `formatRelativeTime`, its `toLocaleString()` calls through `formatNumber`, and every place that shows an error's message, whether through `alert()`, `message.error()` or its own error state, through `translateApiError`;
- its links to databasus.com pages go through `getWebsitePageUrl`;
- it has a lint entry using the options from 2.1;
- `pnpm format` leaves no changes, and `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test` and `pnpm build` pass;
- the reviewer subagent has audited the slice's diff and every CHANGES REQUIRED finding is resolved.
